Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ReadFlex handles information when you use the app.

Information you provide

ReadFlex may store books, comics, documents, article URLs, extracted article content, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, dictionary entries, collections, and app preferences that you add or create while using the app.

Local app data

Reading content and app data may be stored locally on your device so the app can provide offline reading and preserve your library state.

Article extraction

When you save a web article, the article URL may be sent to an extraction service so ReadFlex can create a clean offline reading version. The original source link may be stored with the saved article.

Third-party services

The app may use third-party services for features such as article extraction, translation, dictionary processing, analytics, crash reporting, or app updates. The exact services may depend on the app version and enabled features.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be shared only as needed to operate app features, comply with law, protect rights, or provide services you request.

Your choices

You can remove imported content, saved articles, and collections from the app. Some information may remain in backups or logs for a limited time depending on platform behavior and service configuration.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes or legal requirements evolve. Updated versions will be published on this page.
